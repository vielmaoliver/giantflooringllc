About Us

Flooring with purpose. Execution without excuses.

At Giant Flooring LLC, we believe every space tells a story—and we build it from the ground up.

Based in Raleigh, North Carolina, we are a commercial flooring contractor specializing in high-performance and architectural flooring systems that meet the highest standards of CSI Division 09.

But beyond technical compliance, there’s one thing we never compromise: the trust our clients place in us.

We build with precision, compliance, and long-term results.

Our work supports projects that shape communities, institutions, and brands across North Carolina and the United States.

You’ll find our flooring solutions in:

  • Healthcare facilities
  • Corporate offices
  • Educational institutions
  • Government buildings
  • Hospitality venues
  • Athletic facilities
  • Industrial and logistics sites
  • Multifamily developments
  • Retail and franchise spaces

In every market, we deliver the same level of technical accuracy, full compliance, and lasting performance.

What sets us apart?

  • We understand plans, specifications, and construction sequencing
  • We adapt to tight timelines and complex site conditions
  • We work fast, clean, and without deviation
  • We carry full insurance, including General Liability, Workers’ Compensation, Commercial Auto, and Umbrella coverage
  • We provide Performance and Payment Bonds upon request

We don’t just install. We coordinate. We deliver. We solve.

Our value lies in how we work: no excuses, no delays, no surprises.

We know what it means to be part of a construction project. That’s why, when general contractors choose us, they get more than a flooring crew—they gain a technical partner who protects the schedule, respects the client, and delivers exactly what was promised.

That’s what we do.
And that’s why our clients come back.

Discover Giant Flooring LLC:

At Giant Flooring LLC, we specialize in professional flooring installation for commercial, institutional, and residential projects across North Carolina and beyond. With more than nine years of field experience, our team delivers precise, on-time, and high-quality results, meeting the highest industry standards.
