OUR COMMERCIAL FLOORING SERVICES

Floor Preparation & Surface Readiness

Floor Preparation & Surface Readiness

Every successful installation starts with a solid foundation.
Giant Flooring LLC provides expert substrate preparation — including self-leveling, moisture testing, and surface repair — ensuring floors meet manufacturer specifications and long-term performance standards.

High Performance Flooring Systems

High Performance Flooring Systems

Expert installation of resilient, rubber, and specialty flooring designed for demanding commercial environments — built for safety, durability, and long-term performance.

Athletic & Sports Flooring

Athletic & Sports Flooring

Professional installation of rubber, turf, and resilient flooring for gyms, recreation centers, and athletic facilities. Each project is executed with precision, speed, and long-term performance in mind.

Rubber Flooring & Specialty Surfaces

Rubber Flooring & Specialty Surfaces

Expert installation of rubber systems such as Nora, Eco-Grip, and Protect-All. Ideal for labs, hospitals, and fitness spaces where safety, sound reduction, and traction matters.

Resilient & Vinyl Systems

Resilient & Vinyl Systems

We install all types of resilient systems including LVT/LVP, VCT, sheet vinyl with flash cove and heat-weld seams, and linoleum (Forbo). Perfect for healthcare, education, and retail environments. These systems require durability and cleanability

Carpet & Textile Systems

Carpet & Textile Systems

Carpet tile and broadloom installation for corporate, education, and hospitality projects.
Our crew manages precise patterns, transitions, and adhesive systems per manufacturer’s specification.

Self-Leveling & Substrate Preparation

Self-Leveling & Substrate Preparation

We handle all phases of subfloor preparation: patching, grinding, moisture testing (ASTM F2170), and self-leveling compounds for perfect flatness and adhesion.

Demolition, Removal & Finishing

Demolition, Removal & Finishing

From demolition and adhesive removal to cove base and transitions, we deliver complete
floor replacement services, ensuring sites are ready on time and compliant with inspections.
